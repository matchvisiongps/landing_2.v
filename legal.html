Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · MATCHVISION / matchvision.cz

This Privacy Policy explains how MATCHVISION collects, uses and protects your personal information when you use our website and purchase our products or services.

1. Who we are

MATCHVISION is a football performance analysis brand run by Thomas and Daniel — Certified Analysts by the FC Barcelona Innovation Hub — operating at matchvision.cz. We provide a personal match-analysis service (The Pro Match Report) and a digital education product (the GPS Football Data Blueprint).

For any privacy question, contact us at team@matchvision.cz.

2. What we collect

Information you provide directly

  • Name, nationality and email when you buy a product or submit a match for review
  • Payment information — processed securely by Stripe. We never store your card details.
  • Match video links, GPS exports and match context you submit for The Pro Match Report — used solely to produce your report
  • Messages and questions you send us by email or through our contact form

Information collected automatically

  • Website usage data (pages visited, time on site) via analytics
  • IP address and browser/device type for security and performance
  • Cookies — see section 6

3. How we use your information

  • To deliver what you bought — the Blueprint PDF, or your personalised match report
  • To send transactional emails about your purchase
  • To send marketing emails only if you opt in — unsubscribe any time
  • To improve our website and services
  • To meet legal obligations

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing.

4. Storage & security

Your data is stored on secure servers with industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption in transit. Payments are handled entirely by Stripe, a PCI-DSS compliant processor. Match video and GPS files submitted for a report are kept only as long as needed to complete it and are deleted within 60 days of delivery.

5. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU or Czech Republic you have the right to access, rectify, erase, port and object to the processing of your data, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these, email team@matchvision.cz — we respond within 30 days.

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies (for the site to function) and analytics cookies (to understand how visitors use the site). You can disable cookies in your browser settings, which may affect some functionality.

7. Third-party services

We rely on Stripe for payment processing and Google Analytics for website analytics, each of which may process limited data as described above.

8. Changes & contact

We may update this policy from time to time; the date above reflects the latest version. Questions? team@matchvision.cz.
